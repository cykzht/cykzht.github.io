Record of Anomaly Incident on Tuixuewang on February 21, 2023

发表于2023-02-22|更新于2025-08-07|英文期刊
|总字数:387|阅读时长:2分钟|浏览量:

Cause

Early in the morning on February 21, 2023, the website experienced a surge in traffic, with the server handling approximately 5GB of traffic but still allowing normal access. Around 10 a.m., some users reported issues with version 1.0.7, encountering set_base_info出错, 状态码为403, and version 1.1.4 had Expecting value: line 1 column 1 (char 0) errors.

At 10:55, an announcement for an “urgent fix” was published.

Process

The cause of the error was due to excessive traffic, resulting in the IP being blocked by ZhiXueZhi.com, leading to a 403 Forbidden error. Simultaneously, with a large number of users accessing the site, the server implemented protective measures to filter out some requests.

Outcome

After the IP was unblocked, the website could be accessed normally, and grades could be queried. Following this incident, a user count control feature was added to the website program. If too many users visit at the same time, they will enter a queuing phase to ensure the normal operation of the website.

Due to uncertainty about the block limit imposed by ZhiXueZhi.com, the website temporarily limits access to 100 users. If more than 100 users access, they will be directed to a queuing page, and once users in front exit, the next user will be given access. Each IP is restricted to a maximum of 10 visits per day or 5 visits per minute to prevent malicious traffic flooding.

What We Want to Say

The developer of this website was a high school senior at the time. Due to the inability to see their grades, they could not properly adjust their study pace. Therefore, they developed the Tuixuewang program during the last year of high school to check their grades and make timely adjustments, ultimately getting admitted to a relatively good 985 university in the college entrance examination. The original author’s program was taken offline after the exam. Our company communicated with the original author, obtained the website’s source code and subdomain usage rights, and continues to help students check their grades. Therefore, we reiterate that the original author no longer maintains the technical aspects of the website, and the website may be completely blocked one day. Use it while you can, avoid excessive promotion, and use it discreetly.
